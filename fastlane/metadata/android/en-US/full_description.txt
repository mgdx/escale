Escale finds your way across town — by public transport, by car, by bike or on foot — and tells you what is actually happening right now: which lines to take, where to change, and how late the vehicle is running.

Type where you are and where you are going. Escale offers several routes, sorted into four tabs: Transit, Car, Bike and Walk.

<b>What you get</b>

<ul><li>Departure and arrival times, total duration and number of changes for every route.</li><li>Live delays and cancellations, and the disruption notices published by the operator.</li><li>Step-by-step detail of a trip: the line and its direction, the platform, every stop along the way, walking and cycling directions, wheelchair access, and whether bikes are allowed on board.</li><li>Shared bikes and scooters shown next to your own bike, with how many vehicles are free at the station and how many docks are left where you arrive.</li><li>A full-screen map with stops and stations.</li><li>The next departures at any stop.</li><li>Home, work, saved places and saved journeys — kept on your phone.</li></ul>

<b>What to know before installing</b>

Escale needs an internet connection and cannot plan a journey offline. It computes nothing itself: it asks a routing server, and it is only as good as the data that server has. By default it talks to Transitous, a public server run by volunteers, which covers a large part of the world — including regions where coverage is partial or missing. You can point Escale at any other MOTIS routing server, your own for instance, from the settings.

Escale does not sell tickets, does not book anything, and does not give turn-by-turn navigation.

<b>Privacy</b>

No analytics, no tracker, no advertising, no Google Play services. Nothing leaves your phone except the requests sent to the routing server you have configured, and nothing is sent while the app is closed: Escale has no background task, no service and no notification. Favourites, home, work and search history stay in local storage; automatic backup is switched off, so they are never copied anywhere else.
